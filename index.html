(01) About the atelier

We believe a building should grow quieter, not older. Varen designs and builds homes the way a cabinetmaker builds furniture — slowly, precisely, and for the people who will still live with it long after the photographs are taken.

Wall section · conceptVaren / D-07

Most buildings are designed by one company, priced by another and built by a third. We do all three — so the person who draws the detail is accountable for how it is built.

  • 01

    One accountable team

    Architects, engineers and site managers work in the same studio, on the same drawings, under one contract.

  • 02

    Designed around the site

    Sun, wind, geology and view are measured before form is discussed. The land sets the brief; we answer it.

  • 03

    Material honesty

    Stone, timber, lime and metal, chosen to age well and to be repaired rather than replaced.

  • 04

    Every detail drawn

    Junctions, thresholds and joinery are resolved on paper at 1:5 — never improvised on site.

(06) Standards

Standards, not slogans.

No borrowed badges. These are the working commitments a studio like Varen would write into every contract — shown here as part of the concept.

S-01

One contract, one team

A single point of accountability from first sketch to final handover — no gaps between designer and builder.

S-02

Open-book costing

Every line of the cost plan is visible — materials, labour, margin — with monthly reports during construction.

S-03

Fixed-fee design stages

Design fees are agreed per stage in advance. You decide to proceed at each sign-off, never locked in.

S-04

Independent structural review

Structural design is checked by an external engineer before construction — a second pair of eyes on every load path.

S-05

Material provenance

Stone, timber and metal are recorded by source in a material passport handed over with the building.

S-06

Twelve months of aftercare

Seasonal visits after handover to adjust, tune and repair — because buildings settle and people's lives change.

A small palette of materials that age with grace.

We prefer materials that can be repaired, re-oiled and re-pointed — and that look better at twenty years than at two.

(07) Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

If your question isn't here, ask us directly — we'd rather answer it now than have it surprise you later.

Ask the studio

Five stages: Discover, Strategize, Design, Build and Deliver. Each stage ends with a defined deliverable — a feasibility memo, a cost plan, a planning application, the building itself — and you decide whether to continue at every sign-off.

A private residence typically takes 18–30 months from first meeting to handover: around 6–10 months for design and planning, and 12–20 months to build. Interiors and landscape projects are usually 6–12 months. We give you a realistic programme at the end of the Strategize stage.

The site address or location, any existing drawings or surveys, a rough budget and timeline, and a sense of how you want to live or work in the space. Photos and references help, but a conversation is enough to start.

Design is charged as fixed fees per stage, agreed before each stage begins. Construction is priced on an open-book basis: you see the real cost of materials and labour plus an agreed margin.
